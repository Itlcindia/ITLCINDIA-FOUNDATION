# **App Name**: ITLC Foundation Hub

## Core Features:

- Homepage Impact Showcase: Display key impact metrics (meals distributed, trees planted, animals helped) prominently on the homepage to build trust.
- Donation Gateway: Facilitate online donations with suggested amounts and a custom amount option, ensuring a seamless donation experience.
- Volunteer Sign-Up: Enable users to register as volunteers through a dedicated form, capturing necessary information for coordination.
- Image Gallery: Showcase real photos and videos of the foundation's activities, categorized for easy browsing (Events, Plantation, Welfare, Animal Care).
- Transparency Reports: Provide access to expense reports, donation usage details, registration certificates, and annual reports to ensure transparency.
- Blog and Updates: Publish the foundation's activities, success stories, and awareness articles to the blog.
- AI-Powered Story Generator Tool: Based on impact metrics, generate a story highlighting the impact of the foundation, using reasoning to decide when or if to include specifics such as donation amounts, volunteer names and stories, or geographical information.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Warm light-orange (#FFB347), representing the charity’s approach to people's wellbeing. The choice reflects compassion, and a proactive, solution-oriented approach.
- Background color: Off-white (#FAF9F6). Offers a clean, soft backdrop to ensure readability and a comfortable viewing experience. It helps in highlighting the orange tones while keeping a positive, neutral environment.
- Accent color: Light orange-red (#FF8A5B) is used to draw attention to critical elements and boost overall involvement.
- Font pairing: 'Belleza' (sans-serif) for headings, creating an accessible aesthetic; and 'Alegreya' (serif) for body text, promoting an engaging reading experience.
- Use clear, universally understood icons for services and quick links.
- Employ a clean, grid-based layout with clear sections for easy navigation.
- Incorporate subtle animations for transitions and loading states to enhance user engagement.
